## Building the Constellation

This is a second example post. By adding this file to the folder, you will notice a **third** glowing star appear in the canvas!

You can keep doing this to populate your site with content. As your posts pile up, they'll create a beautiful constellation of everything you've written.

### Cool Features
- The stars pulsate gently
- They are interactable and link to the actual blog content
- Everything is completely static and insanely fast!

Enjoy building your portfolio!
